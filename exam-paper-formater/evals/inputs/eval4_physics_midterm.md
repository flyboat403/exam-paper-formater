# Grade 9 Physics Midterm (question draft)

Full marks: 90, Time allowed: 60 minutes

## I. Multiple Choice (8 questions, 3 points each, 24 points total)

1. The unit of electric current is ( )
   A. volt　　B. ampere　　C. ohm　　D. watt

2. Which of the following materials is a good conductor?
   A. rubber　　B. glass　　C. copper　　D. plastic

3. In the circuit shown, the battery voltage is 6 V and the resistance is 3 Ω.
   The current through the circuit is ( )
   A. 2 A　　B. 3 A　　C. 9 A　　D. 18 A
   [Figure: a simple series circuit — 6 V battery, switch S, 3 Ω resistor, ammeter; label each component]

4. Two resistors of 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in series. The total resistance is ( )
   A. 2.4 Ω　　B. 5 Ω　　C. 10 Ω　　D. 24 Ω

5. A 60 W lamp is switched on for 5 hours. The energy consumed is ( )
   A. 0.3 kW·h　　B. 3 kW·h　　C. 30 kW·h　　D. 300 kW·h

6. The direction of current in an external circuit is ( )
   A. from the negative terminal to the positive terminal
   B. from the positive terminal to the negative terminal
   C. randomly　　D. no direction

7. A wire with resistance R carries a current I. If the voltage across it is doubled
   and the resistance is unchanged, the new current is ( )
   A. I/2　　B. I　　C. 2I　　D. 4I

8. In a household circuit, appliances are connected in ( )
   A. series　　B. parallel　　C. either way　　D. none of the above

## II. Fill in the Blanks (6 questions, 3 points each, 18 points total)

9. Ohm's law states that the current through a conductor is __________ to the
   voltage across it and __________ to its resistance.

10. A 10 Ω resistor has a current of 0.5 A flowing through it. The voltage across
    it is ______ V.

11. The unit of electrical energy in household meters is the __________
    (symbol: ______).

12. A "220 V 1100 W" rice cooker works at its rated voltage. The current through
    it is ______ A.

13. Electric power is defined as the rate of ______ per unit time.

14. The fuse in a circuit melts when the current becomes __________ than the
    rated value, protecting the circuit from __________.

## III. Free Response (2 questions, 24 points each, 48 points total)

15. (24 points) In the circuit shown, a 12 V battery is connected to two
    resistors R1 = 8 Ω and R2 = 4 Ω in series.
    [Figure: series circuit — 12 V battery, switch S, R1 (8 Ω), R2 (4 Ω),
    ammeter A in the loop, voltmeter V across R1]
    (a) Find the current in the circuit. (8 points)
    (b) Find the voltage across R1. (8 points)
    (c) Find the total power consumed by the circuit. (8 points)

16. (24 points) An electric motor is connected to a 220 V supply. Its coil
    resistance is 2 Ω and the current is 5 A.
    (a) Find the electric power consumed by the motor. (8 points)
    (b) Find the heat produced in the coil in 10 s. (8 points)
    (c) Find the mechanical work done in 10 s. (8 points)
